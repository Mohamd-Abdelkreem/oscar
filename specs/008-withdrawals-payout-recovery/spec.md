## Clarifications

### Session 2026-10-08

- Q: C1 - When evidence conclusively proves a payout failed or cannot execute/succeed and no live attempt remains, should P08 release the request or create a replacement payment? → A: Under the owner's delegation to choose the simplest recommended project option, automatically close it as safely failed and atomically release its original gross/source allocation once, charging no platform fee. A later payment requires a new withdrawal under current eligibility/fee rules and a new 72-counted-hour schedule. P08 has no replacement-payment workflow; incomplete proof remains active/reserved and escalated.
- Q: C2 - May an older pending first-address link still save a recipient after a new proof is issued? → A: No. Keep one current pending proof per employee; each accepted issuance/resend supersedes all older unconsumed proofs. Only the latest committed valid proof may save its exact first recipient. Email failure does not revive older links; successful save clears pending proof authority and prevents every later employee overwrite.

## User Scenarios & Testing _(mandatory)_

### User Story 1 - Confirm a first fixed recipient (Priority: P1)

An employee proves their first withdrawal destination through an email link before any withdrawal can reserve money for that recipient.

**Why this priority**: A fixed confirmed destination prevents a forged request or raced link from redirecting employee funds.

**Independent Test**: Request and consume address-bound confirmation links for separate employees, including expired/replayed/competing links; inspect the single saved destination/version without initiating a payout.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an eligible employee with no saved withdrawal destination, **When** they request confirmation for a valid configured-network address and complete the user/address-bound email proof, **Then** exactly that first address is saved once and becomes available to quote creation.
2. **Given** an invalid, expired, already consumed, wrong-user or wrong-address link, **When** confirmation is attempted, **Then** no destination is saved or replaced and no withdrawal/credit/reservation is created.
3. **Given** a pending proof and a newer accepted issuance/resend, **When** either link is consumed, **Then** only the latest committed valid proof can save its exact recipient. If consumption wins before a competing issuance, that first saved recipient remains fixed; if issuance wins first, the superseded link is denied. At most one first destination is committed, and every later employee overwrite is denied; administrator replacement remains P10.
4. **Given** email delivery failure or a lost confirmation response, **When** the employee retries or observes their destination, **Then** the system reports the actual saved/pending outcome, preserves one-save/link safety and never treats provider acknowledgement alone as completed user proof. Failed delivery does not restore superseded proof authority; a new accepted resend issues the new sole valid proof without overwriting a saved destination.

### User Story 2 - Reserve an exact eligible gross amount (Priority: P1)

An employee receives server-calculated terms and accepts one withdrawal whose gross is safely reserved without losing source ownership.

**Why this priority**: Acceptance is the money boundary and must exclude locked or already reserved funds under every competing action.

**Independent Test**: Accept/replay quotes with paid and Free/expired accounts and race withdrawal requests against each other and package purchases; inspect request, ledger and all available/reserved sources. No payout is needed to prove gate A.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** 100 eligible USDT and the initial 21% fee, **When** a 100 USDT request is accepted, **Then** gross 100 is reserved, fee 21/net 79 and the original recipient, fee/eligibility/source/schedule terms are saved; total ownership is unchanged until settlement.
2. **Given** 70 non-referral and 30 referral available USDT with an active paid subscription, **When** 80 is accepted, **Then** 70 non-referral and 10 referral are reserved, 20 referral remain available, and neither reservation source can fund a purchase.
3. **Given** a Free/expired employee with those same sources, **When** they request more than the 70 eligible non-referral USDT, **Then** acceptance is denied with no partial reservation; retained referral funds still belong to them and remain purchase-eligible. Paid reactivation affects new withdrawal eligibility only.
4. **Given** two devices, repeated acceptance or a competing purchase, **When** commands race or replies are lost, **Then** one permitted atomic result wins without negative balances, duplicate reservations or a second active request; observing/replaying the accepted operation returns its saved outcome.
5. **Given** boundary/invalid amounts, stale quote terms, a changed destination or newly blocked account, **When** acceptance is attempted, **Then** inclusive 16/500 limits and exact precision/current authority are enforced and rejected commands leave money unchanged.
6. **Given** an accepted paid request or fee/configuration snapshot, **When** the subscription expires or future rates change, **Then** the original request, including its referral reservation and accepted fee/net, continues unchanged.

### User Story 3 - Schedule and safely stop unsent work (Priority: P1)

Employees receive a durable Baghdad deadline, while administrators may extend it or safely reject unsent work without losing money or redirecting a live request.

**Why this priority**: Durable timing and a single cancellation outcome prevent early sends, forgotten reservations and payment-plus-refund races.

**Independent Test**: Use a controlled Baghdad clock, actual durable scheduling/queue lifecycle and concurrent connections to exercise extensions, restrictions, missed wakeups and cancellation-versus-claim; group B does not begin until these outcomes pass.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** Friday 12:00 Baghdad acceptance, **When** 72 weekday hours are counted, **Then** the deadline is Wednesday 12:00; Saturday/Sunday hours are excluded and weekday overnight hours are included. A weekend acceptance accumulates counted hours from Monday.
2. **Given** a deadline exactly at Saturday 00:00 Baghdad, **When** a new payout is considered, **Then** its earliest dispatch is Monday 00:00, while original counted-deadline facts remain distinguishable from weekend dispatch eligibility.
3. **Given** an unsent scheduled request, **When** an authorized administrator adds positive counted hours with confirmation/reason, **Then** the saved deadline is extended from its existing value and stale due work cannot dispatch at the old time. Zero/negative increments, wrong authority and stale versions produce no change.
4. **Given** safely unsent scheduled work, **When** an administrator rejects it or an account ban/withdrawal block cancels it, **Then** its original source allocation is released once, with reason/server actor/time and final request state committed together; repeated rejection/release adds nothing.
5. **Given** acceptance committed but queue publication is lost, a worker crashes or Redis restarts, **When** the durable scan/worker resumes, **Then** accepted work is found and its current deadline/state is rechecked without losing or duplicating the reservation.
6. **Given** a cancellation, extension or future administrator address replacement racing a claim, **When** one transition wins, **Then** either safe unsent cancellation/extension prevents the old send or possibly sending work remains active/reserved for original-attempt reconciliation; no outcome combines payment and refund or changes the accepted recipient.

### User Story 4 - Automatically pay and settle the original net (Priority: P1)

After gate A passes, eligible due withdrawals pay their fixed recipient automatically and complete only after independently verified successful final evidence.

**Why this priority**: Automatic dispatch fulfils the approved service, while immutable intent and exact settlement prevent employee underpayment and duplicate sends.

**Independent Test**: Exercise due requests through protected test signing/provider boundaries and canonical receipt validation; inspect persisted attempts and complete financial settlement. Add separately opted-in actual payout testnet evidence.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** gate A and due eligible work with company-funded liquidity/resources and admitted protected signing authority, **When** dispatch executes, **Then** the fixed configured USDT treasury sends only the accepted net to the original recipient, automatically and without administrator approval.
2. **Given** a request intent with mismatched owner/request/network/token/source/recipient/amount or an unauthorized signer call, **When** signing is attempted, **Then** it is denied without transfer or financial settlement; no browser/public API can export a key or submit an arbitrary signing payload.
3. **Given** a signed payout, **When** broadcasting becomes possible, **Then** its immutable signed attempt, identity, transaction ID and broadcast intent already have durable recoverable evidence; a crash/lost reply cannot erase the original payment identity.
4. **Given** a successful canonical-final transfer of net 79 for gross 100/fee 21, **When** completion is observed repeatedly or concurrently, **Then** reservation consumption, exact 79 payout plus 21 fee, final status and audit settle atomically once, with no second debit or employee network-cost deduction.
5. **Given** a broadcast acknowledgement, unfinalized/disappearing candidate, malformed receipt, wrong transfer details or failed execution, **When** reconciliation observes it, **Then** it cannot report successful payout or settle; missing evidence retains uncertainty until an authoritative safe outcome is established.
6. **Given** a future fee change, later account/address change or an emergency dispatch pause, **When** an already potentially sent transfer successfully finalizes, **Then** its original recipient/net/fee is reconciled, including during weekends/pause, without redirecting, refunding or changing its accepted terms. A conclusively failed/non-executable outcome instead follows the original-source release rule in FR-036.

### User Story 5 - Recover uncertainty without a second payment (Priority: P1)

After gate A, employees retain truthful active status and their reservation through uncertain sends, while protected recovery reconciles original attempts before financial operation resumes.

**Why this priority**: A timeout or restore must never create a duplicate payout, guessed refund or lost wallet history.

**Independent Test**: Inject crashes/lost signer and broadcast replies, duplicate workers, provider outages and isolated restore using independent recovery records; assert the original transaction identity, reservation and reconciled source totals before resumption.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a transfer accepted by the network but a lost broadcast reply, **When** the worker/signer restarts or retries, **Then** the same durable attempt is reconciled or safely reused, the request stays active/reserved, and neither a new payment nor refund is created from the timeout.
2. **Given** UNKNOWN or any possibly sent state, **When** the employee submits another request or an administrator bans/blocks/changes the address, **Then** one-active-request protection and the original reservation/recipient persist until safe reconciliation; account changes grant no in-flight refund authority.
3. **Given** evidence conclusively proving the original payout failed or cannot execute/succeed and no live attempt remains, **When** reconciliation records that outcome, **Then** the system automatically closes the request as safely failed and releases its original gross/source allocation once with no platform fee. A later payout requires a new accepted withdrawal under current eligibility/fee rules and a new 72-counted-hour schedule. Without conclusive evidence, the original request remains active/reserved and escalated; no replacement payment is created.
4. **Given** insufficient company liquidity/resources, an unavailable provider, missing protected authority or emergency pause, **When** due work is scanned, **Then** no unsafe new send or fabricated failure/refund occurs, basic safe diagnostics identify the blocker and accepted obligations remain discoverable. Detected liquidity/resource shortages produce a bounded operator-visible alert without exposing secrets or creating an employee notification product.
5. **Given** restored financial/request/attempt state, including an older snapshot, **When** protected recovery is performed, **Then** writes/new dispatch remain fenced until post-snapshot financial history and withdrawal/attempt inventory are recovered and original chain outcomes reconciled; ordinary processes cannot approve their own restored authority.
6. **Given** unavailable history, keys, durable attempt material or conflicting recovery evidence, **When** recovery cannot prove authoritative state, **Then** the fence remains, the gap is reported and balances/payouts are not guessed. P11 still owns deployed full-system WAL/disaster/release proof.

### Edge Cases

- Exactly 16/500 USDT gross versus one micro-USDT outside the limits; zero, negative, exponent/grouping syntax, excessive fractional precision, unsupported storage/time ranges and fee rounding at one micro-USDT.
- No confirmed destination; concurrent first-address issuance/consumption, superseded/expired/replayed links, out-of-order email delivery, failed delivery without reviving an old proof, cross-user ownership, lost save responses and later employee attempts to edit a saved destination.
- Paid eligibility at its exclusive expiry boundary; retained referral funds, task-only versus withdrawal-only restrictions, insufficient eligible funds despite a larger displayed total, and pending task rewards that never become spendable before approval.
- Same idempotency key with changed payload; repeated business acceptance under another key; simultaneous purchase/request/approval/deposit effects without overspend or source misclassification.
- Late scans and exact weekday/weekend boundaries; duplicate/stale jobs; extensions/rejection versus claims, ban/block versus signing, and future address replacement without live recipient redirection.
- Crashes before/after signing persistence, broadcast intent and actual broadcast; lost signer/broadcast acknowledgements; invalid/nonfinal receipt, proven execution failure versus unresolved outcome and duplicate final observations.
- Liquidity/resource caps, provider failure, emergency pause, shutdown/restart and restored stale authority; weekend confirmation of already sent work and missing post-snapshot history that must retain the fence.

## Requirements _(mandatory)_

### Functional Requirements

#### Reservation and weekday scheduling - Group A

- **FR-001**: The feature MUST retain both P08 backend groups in one scope. All reservation/scheduling acceptance must pass before payout/recovery work starts; both gates must pass before any P09 frontend integration.
- **FR-002**: All withdrawal amounts MUST use exact bounded integer micro-USDT and validated canonical USDT decimal strings at external boundaries. Percentages MUST use integer basis points and floor to one micro-USDT; invalid precision/range/sign/syntax must be rejected without effects.
- **FR-003**: Each employee operation MUST enforce current identity/session, active verified employee account status and ownership. Initiation/claim MUST enforce relevant withdrawal restrictions; a withdrawal-only block must not conceal an otherwise authorized employee's own accepted-request/history facts. Administrator operations MUST enforce current active ADMIN authority, permitted state and required confirmation/nonblank reason, deriving audit actor/time on the server. A task-only block does not itself block withdrawals; a ban or withdrawal block prevents initiation/claim. Mutable authority must be rechecked at acceptance/claim.
- **FR-004**: Employees MUST be able to observe their own destination readiness, eligible balances, quote/original acceptance outcome and bounded withdrawal history/detail. Authorized administrators MUST be able to inspect bounded request/history facts needed for rejection/extension. Reads MUST distinguish sources, current state, original recipient/version, gross/rate/fee/net, saved deadline, server time/remaining counted hours, and safe transaction/finality facts, without exposing private keys, signed payloads, credential tokens or another employee's private data.
- **FR-005**: An employee with no saved withdrawal address MUST be able to request a bounded single-use email confirmation bound to that employee, action and exact valid TRON recipient. Keep one current pending proof per employee: each accepted new issuance/resend MUST supersede every older unconsumed proof before delivery is attempted. Rejected/invalid issuance changes no proof authority. A link/employee command must not supply authority for another account or address; confirmation must not occur merely by following a read-only URL.
- **FR-006**: Consuming the latest committed valid unexpired address proof MUST atomically save at most one first destination with its version, consume the proof and clear remaining pending proof authority. Superseded/expired/replayed/mismatched links or concurrent competing issuance/consumption MUST NOT overwrite a saved destination. The winning commit governs which proof remains valid; employees MUST NOT change the destination afterward.
- **FR-007**: Address email failures/retries and lost confirmation responses MUST preserve existing saved state and the sole-current-proof rule, expose a truthful pending/saved/error result and not create a withdrawal/reservation. Failed delivery MUST NOT restore an older superseded proof; permitted resend issues a new current proof. Sensitive links MUST remain protected from logs, ordinary diagnostics and retained public state.
- **FR-008**: P08 MUST provide a safe withdrawal-cancellation boundary for future administrator destination replacement. Replacement MUST never redirect an accepted request; its endpoint/UI belongs to P10 and is not implemented by this phase.
- **FR-009**: The system MUST produce a server quote showing gross, applicable fee rate/fee/net, inclusive configured limits, confirmed recipient/version, eligible source breakdown, eligibility basis, Baghdad schedule policy and calculated timing. Quotes are previews; acceptance determines the saved reservation/deadline and must revalidate current facts.
- **FR-010**: Fee selection MUST preserve the approved active subscription's snapshotted withdrawal fee terms; Free/expired accounts use the applicable configured Free terms. All initial rates are 21% (2100 basis points). Future configuration changes MUST NOT rewrite subscription snapshots or accepted withdrawal terms. P08 must support the policy facts it needs without adding P10's broad settings administration.
- **FR-011**: Initial acceptance MUST allow gross from 16 through 500 USDT inclusive, subject to available eligible funds, confirmed destination and current authority. Unsupported amounts or invalid gross/fee/net combinations MUST fail without partial financial effects. Initial bounds remain configurable for future requests.
- **FR-012**: Fee MUST be deducted from gross: fee = floor(gross micro-USDT multiplied by applicable basis points divided by 10,000), net = gross minus fee. The company MUST pay resource/network costs separately; they MUST NOT reduce employee net or create an additional employee deduction.
- **FR-013**: Acceptance MUST derive financial authority on the server and reject unsupported authority fields. A stale/changed quote, membership, balance, restriction, policy or destination must be revalidated or rejected before reservation; different material terms MUST NOT be silently accepted on behalf of the employee.
- **FR-014**: Acceptance MUST atomically commit the withdrawal, gross reservation, exact source allocation, immutable accepted terms/eligibility, ledger/source projections, deadline and required audit. Any dependent failure MUST leave none of those financial/domain effects committed. Total ownership remains unchanged by reservation and reserved money is unavailable to other spending.
- **FR-015**: Acceptance and financial administrative commands MUST bind idempotency to authenticated actor, operation, key and validated intent. Same-key/different-payload commands conflict; replays or lost replies yield the original outcome or truthful in-progress result. Accepted business identity MUST prevent repeated effects even with another/missing client key.
- **FR-016**: At most one active withdrawal MUST exist per employee across devices and concurrent requests, including SCHEDULED, SIGNING, SUBMITTED and UNKNOWN categories and any state in which transfer execution remains possible. Only confirmed completion or safe terminal rejection/failure with release permits another request.
- **FR-017**: New withdrawals MUST use unreserved non-referral funds and, only while actively paid at acceptance, unreserved referral funds. Free/expired accounts retain but cannot newly withdraw referral funds; their eligible deposits/approved rewards remain withdrawable. Pending submissions are not funds. Eligibility MUST use the exact exclusive subscription expiry boundary, not a timely expiry job or browser state.
- **FR-018**: A withdrawal MUST reserve its entire gross from eligible non-referral funds first, then eligible referral funds, recording the identical allocation for release/settlement. It MUST neither consume another reservation nor reclassify referral funds. Competing package purchases retain their separate referral-first unreserved spending rule.
- **FR-019**: Saved gross/rate/fee/net, recipient/address version, source allocation, original eligibility and schedule policy MUST remain immutable. Later subscription expiry, upgrade/reactivation or future policy edits MUST NOT cancel or reinterpret an already accepted eligible request. Permitted extensions append audited schedule changes without replacing the original policy.
- **FR-020**: Initial delay MUST be 72 counted hours in server-authoritative Asia/Baghdad, counting all Monday-Friday hours and excluding Saturday/Sunday. The accepted instant and original counted deadline MUST be retained independently of host/browser time; weekend requests accrue counted time from the next Monday.
- **FR-021**: New dispatch MUST never occur before the current counted deadline or within a blocked weekend. A deadline exactly at the start of Saturday normalizes earliest new dispatch to Monday 00:00 Baghdad. Already sent transfers continue confirmation/reconciliation every day.
- **FR-022**: Accepted state, due time, schedule version, reservation and attempt identity MUST be durable financial records. Periodic scans MUST discover/recover eligible work when queue wakeups are missing, delayed or duplicated. Every wakeup MUST reread authoritative state/deadline/version; queue loss/restart cannot erase acceptance or authorize a new payment.
- **FR-023**: Claims MUST atomically choose one current transition under duplicate/concurrent workers and recheck timing, account restrictions, original destination/version, safe state and dispatch admission. Late or stale jobs cannot bypass an extension/cancellation/pause; expired claims/crashes cannot grant another signer a fresh independent payment.
- **FR-024**: An authorized administrator MUST be able to extend a safely unsent scheduled request by positive counted hours added to its existing deadline with confirmation/reason. The original 72-hour basis MUST NOT reset; zero/negative/unsupported increments, stale concurrent edits and changes after sending may have started must be denied without effect. Saved schedule changes MUST make old due work harmless.
- **FR-025**: An authorized administrator MUST be able to reject only a safely unsent scheduled request with confirmation/reason. Ordinary payouts MUST require no administrator approval. There MUST be no obsolete hold action or 24-hour post-completion cooldown.
- **FR-026**: A ban, withdrawal block or future administrator destination replacement MUST safely cancel an unsent scheduled request through the same protected transition. Existing restriction changes MUST coordinate with withdrawal claims rather than merely setting a flag. Subscription expiry alone MUST NOT cancel it.
- **FR-027**: Safe rejection/cancellation MUST atomically release the identical original gross/source breakdown exactly once with final state, ledger/projections and required audit. Repeating it MUST add no money. Released referral funds retain referral classification and remain withdrawal-ineligible while Free/expired.
- **FR-028**: Once signing/sending may have started, a rejection, ban, block, address change or race MUST NOT release money or claim cancellation from account state alone. The immutable original attempt/recipient remains active and reserved for reconciliation. Either cancellation wins safely before claim or potential send wins with no refund; payment plus release is forbidden.

#### Automatic payout and recovery - Group B, after gate A

- **FR-029**: Due eligible requests MUST automatically dispatch their accepted net USDT from the configured company treasury to the original confirmed recipient when execution preconditions pass. Protected recoverable treasury signing authority MUST be available without exposing/importing custody secrets through the public application or browser.
- **FR-030**: Signing MUST accept only authenticated durable request intents with fixed operation/request/owner, configured network/token, treasury source, recipient/version and net amount, plus independent treasury/resource limits. Forged/mismatched intent, arbitrary signing/key export, missing authority and unsafe network configuration MUST fail closed without a transfer.
- **FR-031**: Before broadcast, the signed attempt, signed material, attempt identity, transaction ID and broadcast intent MUST be durably persisted and recoverable inside the protected custody boundary. Lost signing replies or process crashes MUST not permit a second untracked attempt. Existing recoverable custody/attempt acknowledgements and secret-isolation controls MUST extend to payouts.
- **FR-032**: Retries MUST reconcile/reuse the original safe attempt and its immutable transaction identity. P08 MUST NOT create a replacement payment; a conclusively failed/non-executable payout follows FR-036, and any later payment is a new accepted withdrawal.
