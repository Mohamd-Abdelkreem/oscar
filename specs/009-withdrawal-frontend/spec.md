## User Scenarios & Testing _(mandatory)_

The presentation decisions are now recorded; stories can be tested independently only after the shared P08 and truthful readiness gates pass. These scenarios describe required future verification; no application tests run as part of SPECIFY or CLARIFY.

### User Story 1 - Review and reserve a withdrawal (Priority: P1)

An employee with a confirmed destination reviews a current withdrawal quote and confirms the exact amount to reserve, fee, net payment and destination before committing funds.

**Why this priority**: The employee must understand the cost and reservation, and repeated or competing actions must not reserve money twice.

**Independent Test**: With a funded eligible account and confirmed destination, review and accept a request, then reload and inspect its saved request and wallet reservation.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an eligible employee with at least 100 USDT of eligible unreserved funds and initial 21% terms, **When** they quote and confirm 100 USDT gross, **Then** they see gross 100, fee 21, net 79 and the fixed recipient; acceptance reserves 100 once and displays the saved schedule without claiming payout completion.
2. **Given** Free/expired membership and retained referral funds, **When** the employee requests a quote, **Then** only eligible non-referral funds can fund a new request; referral ownership remains visible and no amount is fabricated as eligible.
3. **Given** a reviewed quote whose destination, fee terms, membership, funds or restrictions materially change and no earlier unresolved acceptance, **When** its first acceptance returns a validated definite stale rejection, **Then** that attempt has no financial effect, its pending guard/recovery handle is retired, the draft is preserved and a fresh authoritative quote must be reviewed if current eligibility permits.
4. **Given** two devices, a double click or repeated confirmation of one request, **When** acceptance races or repeats, **Then** at most one active request and one gross reservation exist; both views converge on the saved winner.
5. **Given** acceptance may have committed but its response is lost, **When** the employee returns or refreshes, **Then** the same quote/request outcome is observed; the UI does not report failure/refund or create an independent replacement while the result is unresolved.
6. **Given** unsupported precision, invalid amount, bounds violation, insufficient funds, unavailable readiness or a missing confirmed destination, **When** submission is attempted, **Then** safe feedback explains the issue and no reservation or local success occurs.
7. **Given** an earlier acceptance remains unresolved or a reply is malformed, lost or obsolete, **When** another error or a live unobserved quote is seen, **Then** the original recovery handle/guard remains; only its committed result or proven expired/uncommitted disposition resolves that uncertainty.

### User Story 2 - Confirm the first withdrawal destination (Priority: P1)

An employee enters their first withdrawal destination, requests its email confirmation and explicitly confirms the same address through the authenticated existing account flow.

**Why this priority**: A payment destination must be confirmed without an accidental save, link-preview write, wrong-account confirmation or self-service replacement.

**Independent Test**: Starting with no saved destination, issue a confirmation, follow the link into the existing account route, review/confirm it and reload the account and withdrawal views.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** no confirmed destination, **When** the employee enters a valid address on the existing withdrawal card and confirms its exact address/network in the reused employee confirmation sheet, **Then** the existing address section shows the saved pending proof/address, expiry and resend timing inline; a request to send email is not described as saved destination or guaranteed mailbox delivery.
2. **Given** the latest valid confirmation link, **When** it is merely opened on the existing account route, **Then** no destination is saved; **When** the matching currently authorized employee reviews the pending exact address and uses its inline explicit confirmation action, **Then** that address is saved once, inline result feedback appears, and creation becomes eligible only if the remaining server conditions pass.
3. **Given** an expired, superseded, replayed or wrong-account link, **When** confirmation is attempted, **Then** the destination is unchanged and safe feedback directs the employee to the current proof or permitted resend.
4. **Given** an unconfirmed address and uncertain/rejected email delivery, **When** the view reloads or resend is requested after the server cooldown, **Then** the current saved proof remains truthful, a newer accepted proof supersedes older links and no extra destination is created.
5. **Given** a confirmed destination, **When** the employee revisits either surface or attempts another save, **Then** the saved destination persists and employee replacement is prevented; admin replacement remains P10.
6. **Given** a destination-confirmation link while signed out or in another account, **When** the flow is visited, **Then** no destination is saved and the proof is scrubbed/dropped without entering retained navigation/history diagnostics; the employee uses the existing login/account-switching flow and reopens the latest email link after matching authentication. The account address section gives safe inline guidance and still requires explicit confirmation.

### User Story 3 - Follow scheduled and uncertain payment states (Priority: P1)

An employee follows the original request, its Baghdad counted deadline and final disposition across refreshes, expiry and provider uncertainty.

**Why this priority**: A timer or transport failure must never become a false completed payment, refunded balance or permission for another request.

**Independent Test**: Observe persisted requests in each lifecycle state under a controlled server clock, refresh across weekends/expiry and verify the matching available/reserved wallet and terminal evidence.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a Friday 12:00 Baghdad acceptance, **When** the employee inspects the existing active card's inline due date, eligible dispatch time and remaining counted hours, **Then** the due date is Wednesday 12:00 Baghdad and counted time does not decrease during Saturday/Sunday; a Saturday acceptance starts accumulating hours Monday.
2. **Given** a due boundary at the start of a blocked weekend interval or an authorized extension, **When** time/status refreshes, **Then** the current counted due date, normalized eligible dispatch time and remaining counted hours agree with server facts; zero remaining time does not assert that money was sent.
3. **Given** SCHEDULED, SIGNING, SIGNED, SUBMITTED or UNKNOWN, **When** the form is opened in any tab/device, **Then** another request remains disabled and handlers cannot bypass that restriction.
4. **Given** a request accepted while referral funds were eligible, **When** the paid subscription later expires, **Then** the original request continues with its accepted sources, fee and recipient; expiry does not invent cancellation/refund or redirect it.
5. **Given** provider unavailability, liquidity/resource shortage, dispatch pause or an uncertain transaction, **When** the employee/admin observes the request, **Then** its actual active state/blocker is shown and funds remain reserved until the server confirms a safe disposition.
6. **Given** confirmed completion or safe rejection/cancellation/failure with release, **When** the employee refreshes, **Then** completion shows actual net payment and accepted fee, while safe release shows the original gross restored with zero charged fee and original source eligibility; a new request has no 24-hour cooldown.
7. **Given** an already sent transfer reconciled on a weekend, **When** final confirmation is recorded, **Then** the view reflects completion without waiting for Monday.
8. **Given** more than one employee history page or a saved request with additional schedule/payment details, **When** Previous/Next or the card's inline read-only disclosure is used, **Then** the matching authoritative page/details appear within the existing history; empty/error/out-of-range results do not fabricate records or enable actions, and no new route/popup opens.

### User Story 4 - Inspect and change safely scheduled requests (Priority: P2)

An authorized administrator inspects real withdrawals and either extends a safely unsent schedule by positive counted hours or rejects it with a reason.

**Why this priority**: Administration must correct unsent requests without redirecting, approving, duplicating or refunding a possibly live payment.

**Independent Test**: Search/filter a real scheduled request, review its detail and confirm an extension or rejection through the existing dialog, then inspect persisted state and employee wallet/status.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an authorized admin and real withdrawal history, **When** the existing search/filter/10-row pagination or a row's inline read-only disclosure is used, **Then** matching authoritative employees, full recipient, money/disposition, original/current counted schedule and saved action actor/time/reason appear within the current table; no local fixture identifies the actor or financial result.
2. **Given** a safely unsent scheduled request, **When** a positive counted-hours extension with reason is reviewed and confirmed, **Then** hours are added to its existing deadline, its original schedule remains inspectable and the updated authoritative schedule replaces any preview.
3. **Given** zero/negative/unrepresentable hours or a blank reason, **When** extension/rejection is attempted, **Then** safe validation prevents the action without partially changing time or funds; supported fractional hours are not arbitrarily limited to integers.
4. **Given** a safely unsent scheduled request, **When** rejection with reason is confirmed or repeated, **Then** one safe rejection restores the identical source allocation once and charges no fee.
5. **Given** another admin extension, a signing claim or loss of admin authority races the open dialog, **When** confirmation uses stale facts, **Then** no unsafe change occurs; current detail is refetched and the operator sees the conflict without losing their reason draft.
6. **Given** signing, signed, submitted or unknown outcome, **When** the row or a stale handler is used, **Then** no reject/refund, extension, manual completion, hold/release or destination redirection is performed. The existing table is read-only for those requests; manual-completion and held/release dialogs, controls and fictional held filtering are absent from the integrated withdrawal surface.
7. **Given** an admin command response is lost, **When** history/detail is reconciled, **Then** the committed action is observed without adding the extension twice or releasing money twice.

### Edge Cases

- Gross exactly at configured minimum/maximum; six-decimal amounts and fee-floor boundaries; excessive precision, exponent/grouped syntax and invalid/empty input.
- Eligibility differs from total ownership: reserved funds and retained referral funds cannot be treated as currently withdrawable; pending task rewards remain outside available funds.
- Quote expiry, exclusive subscription expiry, destination/version changes and purchase-versus-withdrawal races between review and acceptance.
- Countdown observes a different browser timezone, skewed browser clock, background/resumed tab, weekend transition, extension or zero duration without authorizing a transition.
- Late responses for a previously open row, employee, page or signed-out session cannot populate a new scope or enable its actions.
- Empty and filtered-empty history, out-of-range page, failed refresh and malformed successful response remain distinct from a genuine zero balance/no active request.
- Closing/reopening a pending dialog or navigation does not cancel a committed command or release its duplicate guard.
- Proof consumption succeeds but its reply is lost; observing the saved destination resolves uncertainty without re-consuming or offering replacement.
- A restriction/address-change event from its owning backend either safely cancels before claim or leaves an in-flight original request reserved; P09 observes this outcome and does not implement P10 administration.
- A user signs in or switches account after a destination link: the old proof is discarded and the latest email link must be reopened; an expired/superseded reopened proof uses the existing inline current-proof/resend guidance, never stored credential recovery.
- Long Arabic labels, exact amounts, addresses and transaction identifiers remain readable/copyable at narrow widths; pending confirmations retain keyboard/focus and dismissal safety.

Inline detail MUST retain the original/current deadlines. Rejection/cancellation/safe failure MUST show the identical recorded gross/source release once and its current source eligibility. ### Key Entities

- **Employee withdrawal eligibility**: Current authority/restrictions, paid or Free membership basis, eligible non-referral/referral funds, locked referral ownership, available/reserved totals and confirmed-destination/readiness facts.
- **Initial destination proof**: Candidate address/network, latest proof generation, expiry/resend timing, truthful delivery status and explicit confirmation result; the secret proof credential is not public history.
- **Confirmed destination**: Employee-owned saved recipient/network, address version and confirmation time; first save is employee-confirmed, later replacement belongs to P10 administration.
- **Withdrawal quote**: Reviewed gross/rate/fee/net, bounds, source funding, eligibility, recipient/version, expiry and counted-time preview; it is not a reservation.
- **Accepted withdrawal**: Immutable money/source/recipient/eligibility snapshots, original/current deadline and dispatch time, version, saved lifecycle, server time/countdown and safe blocker; terminal evidence is either confirmed settlement or source-identical zero-fee release.
